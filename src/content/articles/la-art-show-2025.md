## Caelis Galeria — **BOOTH 705**

**February 19 – 23, 2025**

**LA Convention Center, South Hall**

### Featured Artists

**August Vilella** · **Arol** · **Marcos Lozano** · **Fulvio Gonella** · **Pedro Hoz** · **Juan Miguel Palacios** · **SNIQUS**

---

Since its inception in 1995, the **L.A. Art Show** has grown into one of the most internationally renowned art fairs in the world. It not only bridges the artistic communities of both coasts but has also established Los Angeles as a pivotal hub in the global art market. Celebrated for its diversity and forward-thinking vision, the fair is dedicated to showcasing contemporary art that challenges conventions and redefines creative expression.

For its 2025 edition, Caelis Galeria presents a curated selection of works by Spanish artists **August Vilella, Arol, Fulvio Gonella, Pedro Hoz, Juan Miguel Palacios,** and **SNIQUS**, alongside newly represented artist **Marcos Lozano**. Together, they offer a compelling body of hyperrealist and primitivist works — inviting viewers into an art world rich with dreamlike imagery, symbolism, and the subconscious imagination.

Over the past three decades, the L.A. Art Show has borne witness to the evolution of artistic expression. Through its **Modern + Contemporary** and **DIVERSEartLA** programming, the fair traces a trajectory from canonical influences to emerging trends. This year, Caelis Galeria continues its commitment to Spanish hyperrealism — not only paying homage to this distinguished tradition, but also affirming its enduring relevance on the global stage.

Long beyond an ordinary art fair, the L.A. Art Show has become a central platform for international artistic exchange, cross-cultural dialogue, and groundbreaking discovery.

Caelis Galeria warmly invites collectors, curators, and art enthusiasts to explore a surreal realm where reality dissolves into dreams. Each painting tells a story — a visual narrative that beckons you into uncharted dimensions.
